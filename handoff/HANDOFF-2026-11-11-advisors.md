# Nov 11: a page for advisors (branch `advisors-2026-11-11`)

Starts from `fixes-2026-11-11` (commit cd4973e, the homepage without scripts), which starts from `main` at dc5ce89. Christian approved the wording, the location and the closing line on Nov 11, and asked for this and the homepage commit to be merged together.

## What it is
One new page in each language for CPAs, estate planning attorneys and family office teams:
- `/asesores/` (file `asesores.html`) and `/en/advisors/` (file `en-advisors.html`).
- Six questions that fall between a family's attorney, CPA and agent (who owns the asset and who the policy names; the teen's license application and the cars; who operates the boat; who employs the household staff; what a hurricane or flood leaves out of pocket; a seat on a board or the family foundation), a three line summary, one closing paragraph with a quiet link to the contact section, and seven sources.
- No new facts. Every factual sentence comes from a guide that is already live (`emp`, `aut`, `mar`, `res`, `flo`, `brd`), with that guide's source. All seven sources were read again on Nov 11. A few sentences were trimmed or addressed to an advisor ("su abogado" became "el abogado de la familia"); no fact, number or statute changed.
- Educational only: no legal or tax advice, no structures recommended, no company named, no promise about results. No form, no call-me strip, no quiz card.

- `tools/flatten.py`: `asesores` and `en/advisors` added to `TOP`; the cleanup loop now reads `TOP` too.
- `index.html` (and `en.html` from the build): one footer link, and one number in the below-the-fold size block. The new link adds a line to the Spanish footer at 360 px, so the 440 px band went from 1517 to 1540. Every width from 300 to 1100 px was measured in both languages before and after; the other five bands still fit. On desktop the English footer now puts its last sentence on a second line, as the Spanish one already did.
