## Needs Christian's call (not edited)

1. col, off-premises: "some companies limit property away from the home to 10 percent of the contents limit [2]". It is sourced (III quote: "Some companies limit the amount to 10 percent"), but it is an insurer-set percentage, which the house rules say not to state. Keep it, or change to "to a smaller share of the contents limit".
2. Every mitigation section ends with "If you want a second look at how your family is set up on this, write to me." It is a soft invitation to contact you, not a sales line. The September pass allowed it; confirm it fits the no-solicitation rule.
3. aut FAQ 2 says UM limits are "equal to BI". The statute says "not less than" BI, which the body uses. This is a simplification, not an error.
