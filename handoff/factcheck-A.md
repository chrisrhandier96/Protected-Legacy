## Trap results

- **cyb, IC3 latest report and year:** VERIFIED. The source is the 2025 IC3 Annual Report (published 2026), the latest available. No 2026 report exists. The text states the year ("in 2025") every time. Every IC3 figure matches exactly: 24,768; $3,046,598,558; 12,368; $275,110,419; 71,843; $1,596,138,595 (> $1.596B); 3,900; $679,013,183; $1,163,919,846; 58%; 89,129; 3,611; >75,000; $449,000. The only fix is how the $449,000 case is described (row 8). Every FTC figure matches the Mar 25, 2026 release: >1M, >$3.5B, 3M, $15.9B.
- **kr, confidentiality condition (no footnote):** Adequately hedged in the body. It says "many" policies, "depending on the wording of each contract" and "if your policy has this condition, confirm with your agent". The advice is defensive only. The summary bullet could add "check yours". No operational security concern.
- **kr, State Dept levels and "K":** VERIFIED. The level names and the K = Kidnapping or Hostage Taking indicator are correct. The page blocks automated fetches (403, Cloudflare bot check, which I did not bypass). Verified from travel.state.gov's own indexed text.
- **kr, III quotes:** All VERIFIED. The one exception is "many current policies add express kidnapping and threats", which is not in III (row 3).
- **kr, FBI PSA I-120525-PSA:** VERIFIED (Dec 5, 2025; title, photo alteration, code word, contact loved one first, report to IC3).
- **kr, PPD-30 / 2015 fact sheet:** Two fixes. (a) The no-concessions policy is "longstanding" and was reaffirmed in 2015. It was not adopted in 2015. (b) The line "no family ... ever prosecuted" comes from the President's June 24, 2015 statement, not from PPD-30 or the fact sheet. The communication and private-efforts points are VERIFIED.
- **str:** DBPR trigger VERIFIED word for word. s. 509.032(7)(b) and the June 1, 2011 exception VERIFIED on the 2026 text (identical to 2025; update the label to 2026).
